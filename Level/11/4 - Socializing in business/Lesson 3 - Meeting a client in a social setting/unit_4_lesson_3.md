#### Perfect tenses
> Use the present perfect tense to talk about an action at an unspecified time in the past.

1. **I've met** him before.

> Use the present perfect continuous to talk about an action that's been going on for some time.

1. **I've been asking** a lot of questions.

> Sometimes the present perfect can also be used to talk about an action that started in the past and continues now. Notice here both sentences have the same meaning.

1. **I've gotten** the runaround for more than two weeks!
2. **I've been getting** the runaround for more than two weeks!

> Just remember that, in contrast to these tenses, the past tense talks about a specific time in the past.

1. We **went** to the bar last night.

### Expressions - Frustration and reassurance
#### Expressing frustration
> There are many ways to express frustration.

1. **I'll be frank:** I'm frustrated.
2. **I almost feel as if** I'm getting the runaround.
3. **People just aren't** being forthcoming.
4. **I've been asking** a lot of questions, but **I haven't been getting** a lot of answers.


#### Reassuring
> If someone is frustrated, you may want to offer reassurance. Use expressions like these:

1. **Listen – rest assured that** I will get some answers.
2. **I can assure you that** things will get better.
3. **I can guarantee that** the situation will change.
4. I will **straighten things out.**
