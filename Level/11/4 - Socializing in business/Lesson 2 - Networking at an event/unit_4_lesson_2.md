## Networking at an event

### Expressions - Networking
#### Making small talk
> Small talk is a good way to start conversations at parties and networking events. Here are some examples.

1. These appetizers are delicious.
2. Are you enjoying the evening?
3. Haven't we met before? You look very familiar.
4. I see you work for Alliance.
5. I'm with Defco. I'm their sales manager.
6. I know him, too. It really is a small world.

### Expressions - More Networking
#### Getting down to business
> After starting a conversation with small talk, you might want to **get down to business**. You can use expressions like these:

1. So, what brings you here?

2. Here – let me give you my business card.

3. I'm actually here to look for a new position in finance.

4. I'm exploring my options.

> Here are some ways to end the conversation, depending on whether you want to follow up with the person.

1. It was great talking to you. I hope you enjoy the evening.

2. Excuse me, but I see an old colleague I need to talk to.

3. Pardon me, but I need to freshen my drink.

4. We really should follow up. Is it okay if I call you?

5. I'd love to talk more about this, at your convenience.

### Reading - Understanding business cards
#### Understanding business cards
> A business card typically includes the person's name, company name and job title. The card also will include contact information, such as address, phone number, email and social networking details.
