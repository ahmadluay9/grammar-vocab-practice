#### Stating considerations
> When you're planning a team-building event, you have to take into consideration the different types of people on the team. Here are some expressions to help you do that.

1. **We have to take into account** people's schedules.
2. **You have to consider that** some people have families.
3. **We have to make allowances for** vegetarians.
4. **Let's not forget that** some people don't drink.
5. **You have to remember that** personal time is very valuable.
6. **We need to take into consideration** the diversity of the group.

> Note that **take into** can be separated.

1. We have to **take** people's schedules **into account**.
2. We need to **take** the diversity of the group **into consideration**.

### Expressions - Making objections
#### Making objections
> When you're about to make an objection, use the following expression to soften your tone.

1. Can I be honest with you?


> To make your objection, use expressions like these:

1. **I have a problem with** anything on the weekend.
2. **I would have to object to** places that allow smoking.
3. **I'm very uncomfortable with** activities with lots of alcohol.

> After objecting, it's a good idea to recommend an alternative.

1. **One thing you might consider is** a fun activity outside.
2. **I recommend** an activity during work hours.
