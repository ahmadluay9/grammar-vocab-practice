# Unit 1, Lesson 2
## Starting a Job Interview

### Vocabulary - Job Titles
#### Titles and positions
> Although companies have different job titles, the person in authority generally starts with the **manager**. After that, the titles rise from director to **CEO** or **president**. Notice that the word **senior** raises the importance of a position.

1. John is a **senior project manager**.
2. She's the **production manager**.
3. He's the **director of design**.
4. Martina is the **vice president of engineering.**

> There are many titles that have a **c** in it. The **c** stands for **chief**. The **CEO** is the highest rank in a company. The other C-titles report to the **CEO**, but are generally above the **vice presidents**. Here are the most common titles.

1. chief executive officer (CEO)
2. chief operations officer (COO)
3. chief financial officer (CFO)
4. chief technology officer (CTO)

#### Finding the interviewer
> The first step is finding the person who is going to interview you. In a large company, you may have to ask an **administrative assistant** or **receptionist**. Be sure to state who you are and why you're there.

1.  - A: Good afternoon. I'm Rob Taylor. I have an appointment with the HR director, Ms. Rifkin.
    - B: Oh, yes. She's expecting you.

#### Greeting the interviewer
> Greet the **interviewer** formally, and thank the person for giving you their time. Listen for clues about how the interviewer prefers to be addressed.

1.  - A: Good afternoon, Ms. Rifkin.
    - B: Oh, please – call me Mary.


2.  - A: Thank you for the opportunity to interview for design manager.
    - B: It's my pleasure. You're a strong candidate.

#### Making small talk
> Expect a short period of small talk. If you came from another city, you may be asked about your trip or what you think about the city.

1.  - A: So how do you like Chicago?
    - B: I love it! Everyone's so friendly!

> The interviewer will signal when it's time to begin the interview.
2.  - A: Well, we only have an hour. Shall we begin?
    - B: Sounds good.
