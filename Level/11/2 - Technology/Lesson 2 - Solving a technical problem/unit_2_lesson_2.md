#### Describing computer problems
> Now and again, you'll encounter technology problems. Here are some words and phrases to help you describe the issues.

1. Oh, no! My computer has **crashed**.
2. You can't log in because your password has **expired**.
3. I can't connect to the internet. Our service must be **down**.
4. If you're having a problem, **reboot** your computer.

#### More multipart verbs for computers
> There are a number of multipart verbs you can use to describe technology issues.

1. I'll sort the problem out.
2. I didn't back up my files, and I had to start all over again.
3. My computer got too hot and shut down.
4. Don't forget to log off from your computer at the end of the day.

### Expressions - Obligation and expectations
#### Expressing obligations and expectations
> Use **be required to** for rules that must be followed. The expression has the same meaning as **have to** and **have got to**, but it is more formal and official.

1. All employees **are required to** change their passwords every month.

> Use **be supposed to** and **be expected to** when something should be done but is not necessarily an absolute requirement. These phrases are similar in tone to **should** and **ought to**.

1. **You're supposed to** back up all the work you do.
2. You're **expected to** tell your manager if you have a computer problem.

> Use **be responsible for** to communicate normal job requirements.

1. Employees **are responsible for** reporting all technical problems to IT.

> Use **be allowed to** to indicate a privilege or right.

1. Employees **are allowed to** work from home one day a week.
