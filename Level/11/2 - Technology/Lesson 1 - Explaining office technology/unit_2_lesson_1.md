#### Talking about technology
> Use vocabulary like this to describe how technology works.

1. Do you know your **user name** and **password**?
2. **Enter** your user name and password to go to the company intranet.
3. You can **access** the company **Wi-Fi** information from the **home page**.
4. Our **intranet** allows us to safely share information within the company.

Language note: The terms **Wi-Fi** and **wireless network** have the same meaning.

### Grammar - Multipart Verbs
#### Multipart verbs for technology
Multipart verbs are verbs followed by one or more prepositions – for example, in, on or down. Type in is an example of a multipart verb.

He typed in the information.


Separable
With some verbs, you can separate the two parts. Notice in the examples that **switch** and **on** are together in the first example but separated in the second example.

1. **Switch on** the computer.
2. **Switch** the computer **on**.


With separable verbs, put pronouns like **it**, **them** and **him** between the verb and the preposition.

1. Switch **it** on.
2. Put **them** in.

#### Inseparable
> For some multipart verbs, the verb and the preposition cannot be separated.

1. **Log in** with your password.
2. You can **double-click on** the icon to open the file.
3. **Scroll down** the page to find the link.
4. You can **zoom in** to make the image larger.
5. With a smartphone, **tap on** the icon to open the program.

### Expressions - Describing how something works
#### How does it work?
> Use these expressions to ask how something works:

1. How does that work?
2. Can you explain how it works?
3. What do I do?
4. How do I do it?

> Use these expressions to describe to others how something works:

1. Let me explain how it works.
2. **First of all**, you have to switch it on.
3. **The next thing** you have to do is log in.
4. **After that**, tap on any icon to open the file.
