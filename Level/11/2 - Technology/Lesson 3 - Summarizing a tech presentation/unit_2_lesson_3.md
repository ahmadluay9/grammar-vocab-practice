#### Vocabulary for innovation: word forms
> Learning the different forms of a word helps you increase your vocabulary and use the word correctly.

- innovate - innovation - innovative
- fail - failure - failing
- emerge - emergence - emerging
- curiosity - curious
- pragmatism - pragmatic
- accuracy - accurate

### Expressions - Expressing Importance
#### Expressing importance
Rather than just saying **this is important**, there are other, subtler ways to express importance that can be just as effective.

##### Stress
> One way to express importance is to stress certain words. In this example, notice the stress on the words **easy** and **when**, and how they convey the importance of timing.

- Predicting the future is the **easy** part. The hard part is knowing **when** it's going to happen.

##### Expressions to add emphasis
Use expressions like the following to help you emphasize an important point:

- **I'd like to talk about** the future.
- **What drives me is** curiosity.
- **The hard part is knowing** who is going to do it.
- **I would look for** a pragmatic person.
- **There's nothing like** hands-on experience.

### Expressions - Making and confirming inferences
#### Making and confirming inferences
> Making an **inference** helps you confirm what other people are saying and reassures them that you understand what they mean. Use expressions like these to make inferences:

1. **It sounds like** you're driven by pragmatism.
2. **I thought she was saying **that we should have fewer meetings.
3. **If I understood him correctly**, we should do more brainstorming.
4. **If I'm hearing you correctly**, only highly adaptable companies are successful.
