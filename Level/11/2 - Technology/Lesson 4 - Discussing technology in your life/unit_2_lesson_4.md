#### Effects of technology
> Here is some useful language to describe the effects of technology on your life.

1. Sometimes all the technology in our lives can be **overwhelming**.
2. I'm totally **dependent** on my smartphone. I'd be lost without it.
3. John is completely **engrossed** in his video game.
4. I'm in a difficult **position**. I don't like technology, but I need it.
5. The **advancement** of technology is unstoppable.
6. **Social networking** has had a very positive effect on my life.
7. We can **access media** in many different ways now.
8. Technology is a **double-edged sword**. It has advantages and disadvantages.

### Debating a point
#### Agreeing and disagreeing
> When debating a point, you may want to show that you agree or disagree with someone.

1. You **have a good point** there.
2. Yes, **I see what you're saying**.
3. I'm **not so sure** about that.
4. I **can't agree** with you there.

> If you disagree with someone, you may want to argue an opposing point.
1. **Actually**, I think technology has made life a lot better.
2. **I hear what you're saying**, but it's a double-edged sword.

> Giving a specific example will make your argument stronger.

1. **I mean**, all I see is kids totally engrossed in their smartphones!
2. **For example**, I know people who can work from home now.

> If you can't resolve your difference of opinion, use expressions like these.

1. We'll just have to **agree to disagree**.
2. Well, **each to their own**, I guess.
